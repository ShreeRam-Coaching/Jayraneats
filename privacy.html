Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

01Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how JYRAN – Tandoor Dining & Lounge (“Jyran”, “we”, “us”) handles your personal information when you visit this website or contact us through it. We are located at C 57, G Block, Bandra Kurla Complex, Bandra East, Mumbai, Maharashtra 400051, inside Sofitel Mumbai BKC. By using this website you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the website.

02Information we collect

  • Booking enquiry details – your name, phone number, and preferred date and time, if you type them into our contact form.
  • Messages and calls – anything you send us on WhatsApp or tell us by phone.
  • Technical information – collected automatically by our website host and by the third-party services this site loads: for example your IP address, browser and device type, pages requested, and an approximate location worked out from your IP address.

We do not ask for payment card details, passwords or government ID numbers on this website.

03How the contact form works

The form does not send your details to a server of ours. When you press “Send on WhatsApp”, the website writes a message from the details you entered and opens WhatsApp on your device. Nothing is sent until you press send inside WhatsApp. After that, the message is handled by WhatsApp (Meta Platforms) under its own terms and privacy policy, and we receive it on our business number.

04How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and to confirm, change or cancel a booking.
  • To give you customer service and to keep reasonable records of bookings.
  • To keep the website safe and working properly.
  • To meet our legal duties.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not send marketing messages unless you ask for them or agree to receive them.

05Third-party services

This website uses services run by other companies. They may receive technical information such as your IP address when your browser loads them:

  • Google Maps – the map in the “Find us” section. See Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts – the typefaces used on the site.
  • Content delivery networks – used to load design code that makes the pages look right.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) – when you choose to send us a message.
  • Website hosting provider – which keeps ordinary server logs.

We do not control these companies and are not responsible for their privacy practices.

06Cookies

Please see our Cookie Policy for details about cookies and similar technologies.

07Who we share information with

We share information only where needed: with our team and, where needed to serve your booking, the hotel in which the restaurant is located; with service providers who help us run the website or take bookings; and with authorities where the law requires it. We never sell your information.

08How long we keep it

We keep booking messages only as long as needed to handle your booking, for reasonable record-keeping, and to meet legal requirements. After that we delete them or make them anonymous.

09Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. No website or message service is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

10Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable Indian law, you may:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct or update it;
  • ask us to erase it, unless we must keep it by law;
  • withdraw consent you have given us;
  • nominate another person to exercise your rights if you cannot; and
  • make a complaint to us, or to the Data Protection Board of India, if you are not satisfied.

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We aim to reply within a reasonable time, normally within 30 days.

11Children

This website is not meant for children under 18. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

12Transfers outside India

Some of the services named above may process information on servers outside India. By using the website you understand this may happen.

13Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. Please check back occasionally.
