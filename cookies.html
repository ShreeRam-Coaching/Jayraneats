Cookie Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

01What are cookies?

Cookies are small files that a website or a service it uses can save on your phone or computer. Similar technologies, such as pixels and local storage, work in a similar way. They can help a website work, remember choices, or measure visits.

02Our use of cookies

This website does not use its own cookies for advertising or tracking, and we do not run analytics or advertising tools on it. We also do not ask you to create an account.

03Third-party services

Some services that appear on our pages may set or read cookies, or collect information such as your IP address, when they load:

  • Google Maps (embedded map) – may use cookies and similar technologies.
  • Google Fonts – may receive your IP address and browser details when fonts are loaded.
  • WhatsApp – when you choose to open it to send us a message, its own policies apply.

These companies decide what they collect. Please read their policies for more detail.

04How to control cookies

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. If you block them, the map or other embedded parts of the website may not work properly.

05Changes

If we start using analytics or other tools in future, we will update this policy and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent first. The date at the top shows when this policy was last changed.
